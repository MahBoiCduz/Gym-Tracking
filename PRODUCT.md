# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Two distinct roles sharing the same app:

**PT (Huấn Luyện Viên):** The personal trainer who builds and manages client roadmaps. Opens the app to create training phases, update plans, review client metrics, and manage multiple clients. Needs to feel in control, professional, and efficient — this tool represents their craft and credibility.

**Học Viên (Client):** Serious kickboxing clients who follow a structured multi-phase program. Opens the app regularly — often on mobile, post-workout or before meals — to check off completed sessions and log food. Primary emotional need: feel empowered, on-track, and that their effort is being counted.

## Product Purpose

A PT management tool that replaces paper plans and spreadsheets with a structured digital roadmap per client. Covers multi-phase kickboxing training plans, session completion tracking, body metrics (BMI, BMR, TDEE), and daily nutrition logging against macro targets.

Success looks like: clients consistently complete sessions week over week, PT can manage multiple clients without friction, and the data tells a clear story of progress.

## Brand Personality

Energetic, precise, motivating. The feel of a coach who is both demanding and personally invested — not a generic platform. Bold where it counts, focused everywhere else. "Driven. Sharp. Yours."

Three words: **Fierce. Structured. Human.**

The emotional tone is that of a great PT session — high energy but purposeful, never chaotic.

## Anti-references

- **Generic fitness SaaS** (MyFitnessPal, Fitbit): Blue-gradient dashboards, stock-photo abs, widget-heavy layouts that feel like a subscription product rather than a personal coaching relationship.
- **Gamer / Esports dark UI**: Neon-on-black, glitch effects, aggressive decorative typography — feels performative rather than performance-focused.
- **Corporate wellness app**: Mint green, soft rounded everything, pastel gradients — feels like a healthcare benefits portal, not a kickboxing gym.

## Design Principles

1. **Coach-tone over system-tone.** Language, hierarchy, and visual cues should feel like a human trainer giving direction — not a software product presenting data. Actions should feel instructed, not optional.

2. **Progress as protagonist.** Completion states, phase advancement, and metric improvement should feel earned and visible. A user should know at a glance whether they're winning or falling behind.

3. **Two-audience clarity.** PT mode and Client mode should feel obviously distinct without requiring explanation. Different roles, different power levels, different visual language — all in one app.

4. **Discipline made visible.** The structure and rigor of multi-phase training plans should read as craft and expertise, not bureaucratic lists. The design amplifies the PT's professionalism.

5. **Energy without excess.** Bold at the moments that matter — achievements, actions, warnings — and focused everywhere else. No decoration that doesn't carry information or energy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target: WCAG AA compliance (4.5:1 body text, 3:1 large text / interactive elements)
- Vietnamese-language UI throughout — font choice must support full Vietnamese diacritic set
- Mobile-first: clients primarily check in on phone post-workout or at meals
- Reduced-motion support required — some users may be on lower-end devices
- Both roles must be usable in low-ambient-light conditions (post-workout, evening logging)
